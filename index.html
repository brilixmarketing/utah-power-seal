Why bother sealing?

Concrete soaks up water like a sponge. Here, that water freezes and thaws over and over all winter, and every cycle chips away at the surface a little more. Add the salt that comes off your tires and you get the flaking and pitting you see on a lot of older driveways.

A good seal slows all of that down. It also makes oil and spills a lot easier to clean up.

Best time to do it: before the first hard freeze, or in spring once nights stay above freezing.
