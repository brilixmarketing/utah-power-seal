Why seal in Utah

Our weather is hard on concrete

Unsealed concrete is porous. Along the Wasatch Front, that means water, salt and sun get to work on your driveway every single year.

Homeowner FAQ

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced by quote because the size, condition and type of concrete varies from home to home. Bids are free. Call 385-448-9466 or send the form above to set one up.

Can you come out after work or on a Saturday?

Yes. We offer evening and weekend bids so you can be there when we look at your property.

Why should I seal my driveway?

Concrete is porous. In Utah, water that soaks in can freeze and expand, and ice melt adds wear on top of that. A good sealer helps keep water and salt out and makes stains easier to clean.

When can I drive on it again?

It depends on the sealer and the weather. We'll tell you exactly when it's ready for foot traffic and vehicles on the day of the job.

Do you only seal, or do you power wash too?

Both. We can power wash on its own, or wash and seal together. Sealing always starts with a thorough cleaning.

What areas do you serve?

Cities throughout Utah County and Salt Lake County. If your city isn't listed, give us a call and ask.
