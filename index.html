Why seal in Utah?

Concrete soaks up water like a sponge. Here, that water freezes and thaws over and over all winter, and every cycle chips away at the surface a little more. A good seal slows all of that down.

Common questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and condition of your concrete, so we quote every job after seeing it. The quote is free.

Can you come after work or on a Saturday?

Yes. We do evening and weekend bids so you can be home when we look at it.

When can I drive on it again?

It depends on the weather that day. We'll give you a time before we leave.

Can I just get it power washed?

Yes. Sealing always starts with a wash, but you can get a wash without the seal.

When's the best time to seal?

Before the first hard freeze, or in spring once nights stay above freezing.
